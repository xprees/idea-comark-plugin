Reports site-absolute image paths in Comark documents that no image directory of the Nuxt project serves.

Checked are image destinations (![alt](/images/hero.webp)) and their reference definitions, and image-like attributes of block and inline components (src, image, cover, heroImage, ...). A path is found in public/ of the app and its layers, in the Nuxt Image directory (image.dir), or in the image directories configured in Settings | Languages & Frameworks | Comark. The app.baseURL, app.cdnURL and /_ipx/ prefixes and any ?query or #hash are ignored.

Example:

![Phishing](/threats/phising.webp)

Remote URLs, image.alias paths and relative paths are not checked, and neither are documents outside detected Nuxt projects. The quick fix replaces the path with the closest existing image path. Image values in the front matter and in props blocks are checked by Missing Nuxt image in front matter and props.
