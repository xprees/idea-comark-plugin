Reports Comark block components (::name) and inline components (:name) that no known component matches.

Components are discovered from .vue files in Nuxt components/ directories, from .nuxt/component-meta.mjs files written by nuxt-component-meta, and from the component metadata URL in Settings | Languages & Frameworks | Comark. The inspection stays silent until at least one component has been discovered.

Example:

::alret{type="info"}
Typo in the component name.
::

Components registered at runtime (by a Nuxt module or a custom renderer) may be unknown to the IDE; the inspection is therefore disabled by default.
