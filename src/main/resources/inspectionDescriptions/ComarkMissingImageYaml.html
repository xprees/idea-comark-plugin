Reports site-absolute image paths in the front matter and in component props blocks of Comark documents that no image directory of the Nuxt project serves.

Checked are values of image-like keys: image, cover, thumbnail, og:image, seo.image, image.src, heroImage, ... A path is found in public/ of the app and its layers, in the Nuxt Image directory (image.dir), or in the image directories configured in Settings | Languages & Frameworks | Comark.

Example:

---
image: /threats/phising.webp
---

Remote URLs, image.alias paths and relative paths are not checked, and neither are documents outside detected Nuxt projects. The quick fix replaces the path with the closest existing image path. Paths in Markdown images and component attributes are checked by Missing Nuxt image.
