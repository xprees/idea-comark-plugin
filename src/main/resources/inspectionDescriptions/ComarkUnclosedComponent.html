Reports block components that have no closing marker.

Comark closes such a component automatically at the end of its parent component (or of the document), so everything up to that point becomes the component's content, which is rarely what was intended.

Example:


::alert
Watch out!

This paragraph ends up inside the alert as well.

The quick-fix inserts the closing marker (::, :::, ... with as many colons as the opener) on its own line after the component's last content line, indented like the opener.
